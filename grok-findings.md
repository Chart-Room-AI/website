Overall Marketing Score: 4/10
Business Conversion Potential: 3/10
Key Strengths

Copy is exceptionally sharp, specific, and pain-first — it instantly speaks the language of regulated financial services leaders (stalled pilots, audit risk, vendor noise, board scrutiny). This is rare and highly effective for the niche.
Credibility is baked in authentically through operator stats (31 years, 18 as CTO, CISO experience) without sounding boastful.
Strong competitive positioning against “generic strategy decks” and “vendor-driven roadmaps” — the site owns the “fractional operator judgment” lane clearly.

Critical Weaknesses

Hero section has zero CTAs. A visitor reads the strongest headline on the site and then… scrolls or leaves. This is a conversion killer.
Zero visual assets — no hero imagery, no icons, no screenshots, no diagrams, no headshots, no client logos. The site feels like a LinkedIn article dumped into a bare template. First impression is cold, generic, and low-energy.
Complete absence of social proof, testimonials, case results, or even named clients. In B2B financial services, trust is everything — this page offers none.
Lead capture is friction-maximized: only mailto links and generic “/contact” pages. No forms, no lead magnets, no low-friction entry points.
Text density is brutal. Cognitive load is high; the page reads like a whitepaper instead of a conversion machine.
No urgency, scarcity, or clear next-step funnel. Visitors are educated but not guided toward a booking or diagnostic call.

Add a prominent, benefit-driven hero CTA (primary + secondary) — e.g., “Get your AI readiness diagnostic in 48 hours” and “Book a 20-min alignment call.” Expected impact: 3–5x increase in contact rate from homepage traffic.
Inject credible social proof immediately — even one anonymized quote or a “worked with” line (without naming if compliance blocks it). Expected impact: massive lift in perceived authority and conversion.

Replace mailto links with embedded Calendly or simple Typeform on-page for the three engagement paths. Expected impact: 40–60% reduction in drop-off between interest and booked call.
Create a dedicated lead magnet (e.g., “7 Questions Every FS Leader Must Ask Before Scaling AI Pilots”) gated behind a simple form. Expected impact: builds email list and nurtures stalled prospects.

Strategic Overhaul Ideas (bigger moves that would significantly move the needle)

Full visual rebrand to premium operator aesthetic: dark navy + gold accents (trust + excellence), clean sans typography, subtle nautical chart line-work integrated with modern AI node graphics. Make it look like the high-end fractional CTO it actually is.
Build a micro-conversion funnel: homepage → AI readiness diagnostic quiz (5 questions) → personalized PDF + Calendly booking. This would turn passive readers into qualified leads.
Add a “Results” or “Recent Engagements” section (anonymized) showing specific outcomes like “reduced pilot-to-production time from 11 months to 5 weeks” or “secured board approval on $X roadmap.”
Create separate landing pages for each engagement path (Diagnostic / Roadmap Sprint / Fractional Leadership) with dedicated hero CTAs and forms — current “one page does everything” approach dilutes conversion power.

This site currently wins on substance but loses badly on marketing execution. The copy is already 9/10 for the niche — the design and conversion architecture are 2/10. Fix the obvious friction points and this becomes a serious lead-generation asset instead of an expensive online brochure.
